# NG2 visual concepts

These are exploratory assets generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow. They intentionally avoid the NGA deer/antler silhouette, NGA lettering, and the project's current `NG` wordmark. Existing production assets were not replaced.

## A. Paper Shards / 纸片对话

- `paper-shards-icon.png` — 1254×1254, RGBA
- `paper-shards-background.png` — 1536×1024, RGB
- Character: playful, closest to the supplied fragmented-paper reference
- Best fit: friendly community identity and light theme

Icon prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square app icon concept for an open-source discussion forum
Primary request: Create an original abstract emblem suggesting both an open discussion thread and an open book, assembled from 6 folded geometric paper shards. The fragments should feel lively and conversational, loosely orbiting and interlocking into one unmistakable centered symbol.
Scene/backdrop: a warm cream circular field filling most of a square canvas, with a clean neutral outer margin.
Subject: one compact abstract mark made only from 6 simple folded paper polygons; use small overlaps and fold facets to imply paper, connection, exchange, and an opening book without depicting a literal book.
Style/medium: crisp flat vector-friendly raster illustration, modern editorial icon, minimal geometry, subtle paper-fold facets, no photorealism.
Composition/framing: square 1:1; centered; strong compact silhouette; generous safe area; evenly balanced; clearly readable when reduced to 48 px.
Color palette: coral red, mustard yellow, sky blue, jade green, and a small pink accent on warm cream. Keep color blocks distinct and saturated but tasteful.
Constraints: entirely original; inspiration is only the playful energy of scattered paper fragments; exactly 5–7 folded shards; no letters; no words; no text; no animals; no antlers; no deer; no NGA logo; no resemblance to an existing trademark; no border decorations; no watermark.
Avoid: gradients, excessive shadows, thin lines, tiny details, confetti outside the emblem, 3D rendering, mockup presentation, app-store badge, glossy effects.
```

Background prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: landscape UI background companion for an open-source discussion forum
Primary request: Create a calm, original abstract background that visually belongs with a playful folded-paper app icon. Use a warm cream base with sparse tiny folded-paper fragments and very faint curved conversation trails, suggesting ideas quietly moving through a community.
Scene/backdrop: uninterrupted warm cream field with a subtle natural paper-like tonal softness, clean and nearly flat.
Subject: a few miniature abstract folded-paper polygons in coral red, mustard yellow, sky blue, jade green, and restrained pink; place them mainly near the corners and outer edges. Add only a handful of extremely subtle thin curved trails like gentle message paths, also confined toward the perimeter.
Style/medium: clean flat vector-friendly raster illustration, minimal editorial graphic, low contrast, soft and refined.
Composition/framing: wide landscape canvas; calm center with generous negative space for UI and readable content; peripheral decoration only; visual weight balanced across corners; seamless-feeling without a central focal point.
Lighting/mood: warm, friendly, quiet, spacious.
Color palette: warm cream dominant; edge decorations use muted coral, mustard, sky blue, jade, and pink at restrained saturation; conversation trails are only slightly darker than the base.
Constraints: no text; no letters; no logo; no watermark; no animals; no antlers; no deer; no recognizable trademark; no UI mockup; no panels; no buttons; center area must remain clean; all fragments must stay small and sparse.
Avoid: busy confetti, dense patterns, strong contrast, dark areas, gradients that distract, photographic texture, large central shapes, borders, frames, shadows, realistic objects.
```

## B. Jade Threads / 翡翠织线

- `jade-threads-icon.png` — 1254×1254, RGB
- `jade-threads-background.png` — 1672×941, RGB
- Character: crafted, calm, closest to the app's current jade-and-cream theme
- Best fit: primary production direction; easiest migration from the current identity

Icon prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square app icon concept for an open-source discussion forum project
Primary request: Create one original modern emblem made from two interwoven rounded ribbons. The ribbons form two overlapping conversation loops while also suggesting a subtle lowercase n-like continuous path through negative space. This must be a newly invented mark, clearly distinct from NGA branding.
Scene/backdrop: warm cream paper field, clean and uncluttered
Subject: a single centered abstract woven-loop emblem
Style/medium: minimal vector-friendly brand mark with a tactile cut-paper and very soft embossed finish; essentially flat, crisp, refined, not photorealistic
Composition/framing: square canvas, emblem centered in a generous safe area with even padding; bold compact silhouette that remains immediately readable at 48 px; balanced negative space
Lighting/mood: soft diffuse studio light, calm, crafted, contemporary
Color palette: dominant deep jade #14796B, warm cream background, exactly one restrained vermilion accent on a small ribbon segment
Materials/textures: subtle paper fiber, extremely shallow embossing, clean edges
Constraints: no letters or text; do not render an explicit letter n, only a subtle path suggestion; no deer; no antlers; no animals; no NGA logo; no existing trademark; no watermark; no border; no mockup device; no extra symbols; no gradients except imperceptible material shading; strong original silhouette; one emblem only
```

The icon received one precise edit to make the background fully opaque while preserving the mark:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: square app icon concept

Primary request: Change only the transparent/black-rendering canvas background to a solid warm cream paper field. Preserve the emblem's exact woven two-loop geometry, scale, position, deep jade color, single vermilion segment, material texture, edges, and shadows.
Scene/backdrop: solid warm cream #F4E8CF with very subtle paper grain, fully opaque all the way to every edge
Constraints: background must be fully opaque warm cream, including inside the loop openings; do not change or redraw the emblem; no text; no letters; no deer; no antlers; no animals; no NGA logo; no trademark; no watermark; no border; no extra objects
```

## C. Night Signal / 夜间信号

- `night-signal-icon.png` — 1254×1254, RGB
- `night-signal-background.png` — 1672×941, RGB
- Character: technical, contemporary, dark-mode-first
- Best fit: a developer/open-source community positioning

Icon prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square app icon concept for an open-source forum reader
Primary request: create an original abstract “night signal” emblem made from exactly three rounded message tiles plus one small star/spark, visually connected in a circular reading flow or orbit. The mark should suggest threaded conversation, discovery, and community without using any existing forum logo.
Scene/backdrop: solid near-black charcoal square field, edge-to-edge, no mockup, no device frame.
Subject: one centered compact emblem; three simple rounded speech/message tiles rotate gently around an implied circular path, with a tiny four-point spark completing the rhythm. Use negative space and overlap carefully so it remains a bold single silhouette.
Style/medium: crisp flat geometric vector-like raster artwork, premium modern app-icon design, minimal, precise edges, with only a very subtle restrained glow.
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, centered, generous safe area of about 18–22% on all sides, no border, readable and distinctive when reduced to 48 px.
Color palette: near-black charcoal background; luminous jade and cyan for the main tiles; one small warm amber accent; no rainbow palette.
Lighting/mood: nocturnal, calm, intelligent, quietly energetic.
Text: none.
Constraints: original design only; exactly three rounded message tiles and one small star/spark; strong silhouette; flat colors; no letters, no words, no typography, no people, no animals, no deer, no antlers, no NGA logo or derivative, no watermark, no UI screenshot, no photorealism, no 3D bevel, no excessive glow, no busy texture.
```

The icon received one precise edit to reduce glow and flatten its rendering:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: square app icon concept for an open-source forum reader
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and approved composition.
Primary request: change only the rendering treatment by reducing the luminous glow by about 75% and making the emblem feel crisp, flat, geometric, and vector-like. Preserve the exact concept: three rounded message tiles, one small amber four-point spark, and the circular reading-flow/orbit arrangement.
Scene/backdrop: keep a solid opaque near-black charcoal square field, edge-to-edge.
Composition/framing: keep the centered mark, generous safe area, proportions, tile count, star count, and circular flow; preserve strong silhouette readable at 48 px.
Color palette: keep jade, cyan, and the small amber accent; slightly restrain saturation only if needed for a premium flat result.
Constraints: change only glow and surface rendering; preserve all objects and overall composition; use flat fills with crisp edges and at most a very thin subtle edge illumination; no large halo, no gradients that imply glass or 3D, no letters, no words, no typography, no people, no animals, no deer, no antlers, no NGA logo or derivative, no watermark, no mockup.
```

Background prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: landscape dark companion background for an open-source forum reader UI
Primary request: a restrained nocturnal “night signal” backdrop suggesting quiet online conversation and connected reading, using sparse abstract constellation/network lines and tiny rounded message-tile sparks.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed charcoal-to-deep-jade very subtle gradient, dark and matte, with faint fine-line network constellations and a few tiny message-tile-shaped sparks concentrated mostly along the outer edges and corners.
Subject: atmospheric abstract background only; delicate points, short connecting arcs, and miniature rounded message-tile glints near edges; no central emblem.
Style/medium: minimal flat/vector-like digital background with slight organic grain, understated and production-ready, not sci-fi concept art.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape; keep the central 60% calm, low-detail, low-contrast, and evenly dark for highly readable UI overlays; edge motifs should feel sparse and asymmetrical, never form a frame.
Lighting/mood: quiet night, focused reading, calm community presence; nearly no glow.
Color palette: near-black charcoal, deep jade, muted cyan traces, extremely rare dim amber pinpoints; overall low saturation and low contrast.
Text: none.
Constraints: no logo, no large icon, no central focal object, no lettering, no typography, no watermark, no people, no animals, no deer, no antlers, no NGA mark or derivative, no starscape photography, no bright nebula, no busy texture, no high-contrast lines, no visible UI, no panels, center must remain empty and readable.
```
