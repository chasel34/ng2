# Jade Foot variants

All three concepts use the previous Jade Threads direction as a style anchor. The supplied footprint images were used only to establish the requested subject: one human footprint. Their silhouettes, toe construction, arch cutouts, fragment layouts, orientations, and colors were explicitly excluded from copying.

## 1. Ribbon Foot

File: `jade-foot-ribbon.png`

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square app/project logo concept
Primary request: Create one original, unmistakable HUMAN FOOTPRINT logo mark, formed by one continuous broad deep-jade woven ribbon that begins as a full rounded heel, flows through the sole, and rises toward the toes. The main footprint body should read as one coherent ribbon silhouette with a gentle S-shaped inner negative space. Add exactly five separate rounded woven-knot toes, clearly human, arranged in a natural but original descending-size arc; use a toe spacing and toe silhouette unlike either semantic reference.
Input images: Image 1 is STYLE ANCHOR ONLY — preserve its warm cream paper background, deep jade woven-ribbon material, subtle embossed cut-paper texture, soft restrained relief/shadows, and one restrained vermilion accent. Images 2 and 3 are SEMANTIC REFERENCES ONLY — they only confirm the subject is one human footprint; do not copy their footprint silhouette, toe shapes or spacing, arch cutout, shard arrangement, orientation, colors, or composition.
Scene/backdrop: fully opaque warm cream square paper background, clean and uninterrupted.
Subject: exactly one human footprint, diagonal from lower-left heel toward upper-right toes; full heel; broad flowing sole; exactly five toes. Include one tiny vermilion stitch embedded at the mid-foot, and no other vermilion elements.
Style/medium: minimal vector-friendly logo rendered as layered woven cut paper; rounded flowing geometry; subtle embossed tactile grain; simple, premium, friendly, recognizable at 48 px.
Composition/framing: centered on a square canvas with generous even safe-area padding; strong silhouette; no cropping; footprint occupies about two-thirds of the canvas.
Color palette: warm cream background, deep jade mark, one small vermilion stitch only.
Materials/textures: refined woven paper texture with subtle edge depth; texture must not reduce small-size legibility.
Constraints: exactly one footprint; exactly five toes; one continuous broad jade ribbon for heel and sole; five rounded jade toe knots; full heel; gentle S-shaped inner negative space; one small vermilion stitch at mid-foot; diagonal upward-right orientation; fully opaque background; no text.
Avoid: copying NGA or either reference silhouette; NGA logo; letters; words; deer; antlers; animal paw; hoof; speech bubbles; fragments; colorful shards; extra footprint; extra toes; missing toes; gradients; busy decoration; mockup; border; watermark.
```

The initial transparent background was corrected with this targeted edit:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: square app/project logo concept
Primary request: change ONLY the canvas background from transparent to a fully opaque warm cream paper background (#F4E4C3 approximate), with an extremely subtle natural paper grain matching the existing cut-paper craft style.
Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve the existing single jade human-footprint mark exactly: same diagonal orientation, same full heel and sole ribbon geometry, same gentle S-shaped negative spaces, exactly five rounded loop-knot toes, same toe sizes and positions, same subtle shadows, same woven texture, same one small vermilion mid-foot accent, and same centered safe-area composition.
Constraints: background must be fully opaque edge-to-edge; change only the background; keep the mark unchanged; exactly one footprint; exactly five toes; no text; no additional elements.
Avoid: transparency; black background; white background; checkerboard; extra toes; missing toes; extra footprint; NGA logo; letters; deer; antlers; paw; hoof; speech bubbles; colorful fragments; border; mockup; watermark.
```

## 2. Dialogue Path Foot

```text

Primary request: Create one original compact logo mark for an open-source NGA-inspired community client. The mark must be a SINGLE HUMAN FOOTPRINT, clearly recognizable as one human footprint while visually distinct from the semantic references.
Input images:
- Image 1 (jade-threads-icon.png): STYLE ANCHOR ONLY. Borrow only its warm cream paper background, deep jade color (#14796B), tactile cut-paper / very soft embossed finish, and exactly one restrained vermilion accent. Do not reuse its motif, layout, geometry, threads, or composition.
- Image 2 and Image 3: SEMANTIC REFERENCES ONLY for the broad idea “one human footprint.” Do not copy either silhouette, toe shapes or spacing, arch, shard layout, orientation, palette, or composition.

Subject: exactly one compact deep-jade HUMAN FOOTPRINT made from one bold rounded sole shape plus exactly five separate rounded teardrop toes. Include a full heel. Make the footprint asymmetric and modern. Arrange the five toes in a lateral arc unlike either reference.
Distinctive construction: cut one clean curved negative-space channel through the sole; it should subtly suggest a threaded conversation/read path, but must NOT become or resemble a speech bubble. Place exactly one small vermilion circular node inside this curved channel, and no other vermilion marks.
Style/medium: minimal vector-friendly brand mark rendered as tactile cut paper with extremely subtle soft embossing; clean strong silhouette; restrained, calm, modern.
Composition/framing: upright footprint with a slight left lean, centered in a generous safe area, balanced padding on all sides, optimized to remain legible and recognizable at 48px.
Color palette: deep jade #14796B mark; warm cream opaque background; exactly one small restrained vermilion circular node.
Constraints: exactly one footprint; exactly one bold rounded sole; exactly five rounded teardrop toes; full heel; exactly one curved negative-space channel through the sole; exactly one vermilion circular node inside the channel; no text or letters; no extra objects; no transparency; no watermark.
Avoid: copying NGA logo; deer; antlers; paw; animal track; hoof; claws; speech bubble; chat icon; the letter N or G; toe/shard layout from references; a second foot; extra dots or decorative marks; mockup presentation; border; icon grid; gradients; shiny 3D.
```

## 3. Folded Foot

File: `jade-foot-folded.png`

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square project app icon concept for an open-source NGA-inspired community client
Input images: Image 1 is STYLE ANCHOR ONLY: use its warm cream paper background, deep jade palette, tactile layered cut-paper softness, subtle grain, gentle dimensional shadows, and restrained vermilion accent. Images 2 and 3 are SEMANTIC REFERENCES ONLY for the subject “one human footprint”; do not copy either footprint silhouette, toe shapes, arch cutout, colorful shard arrangement, orientation, or colors.
Primary request: Create one distinctive, unmistakable HUMAN FOOTPRINT logo mark, newly designed and clearly different from both semantic references. Construct the sole from ONLY THREE large interlocking folded-paper planes: one heel plane, one broad connecting arch plane, and one forefoot plane. Use three closely related deep jade tones. Add EXACTLY FIVE small rounded folded-paper tabs as human toes, graduated naturally in size and visibly attached/closely integrated with the forefoot so the whole mark reads as one cohesive footprint, not scattered fragments.
Style/medium: premium tactile layered cut paper, rounded friendly geometry, compact vector-friendly logo silhouette, minimal detail.
Composition/framing: one footprint only, centered with generous safe area, oriented diagonally upward-left (toes at upper-left and heel at lower-right), strong readable silhouette at 48px. The heel, arch, and forefoot must form a connected broad shape rather than a hollow ring or separated shards.
Color palette: nearly monochrome deep jade, using exactly three closely related jade tones for the three sole planes; exactly one thin vermilion fold line as the only warm accent.
Scene/backdrop: fully opaque warm cream square background with subtle fine paper grain.
Lighting/mood: gentle shallow paper-layer shadows, calm, crafted, contemporary.
Constraints: exactly one human footprint; exactly three large interlocking sole planes; exactly five rounded toe tabs; exactly one thin vermilion fold line; no text or letters; no extra symbols; no border.
Avoid: copying NGA’s silhouette; copying the colorful reference; no scattered shards; no sharp triangular geometry; no animal paw, hoof, deer, antlers, claws, or paw pads; no NGA logo; no speech bubbles; no infinity symbol; no watermark; no extra footprint; no device mockup; no transparent background.
```
